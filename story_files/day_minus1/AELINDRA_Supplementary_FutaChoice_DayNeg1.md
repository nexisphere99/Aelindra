## THE SCENE

Late afternoon light. The covered bridge between the palace and the tower. Edrin walks it for the second time today. His arms ache from two hours of beating straw men. His palms are blistered. His shirt is sweat-damp.

The tower entrance. No Ilara waiting this time — he didn't announce himself. He climbs the stairs. Finds her in the Archsorceress's quarters, writing at a desk, candles lit against the fading daylight.

She looks up. No surprise — she may have been expecting this.

> **EDRIN** (standing in the doorway, not entering, his voice hoarse from exertion and emotion): "Is that the only way?"

Ilara sets down her quill.

> **ILARA:** "Sit down, Prince Edrin."
> **EDRIN:** "Just answer me. The Rite you described this morning — bones reshaping, cock dissolving, the whole... REWRITE. Is that the ONLY form? Is there any version that doesn't require losing... everything?"

Ilara studies him. The clinical composure shifts — just slightly — into something more careful. She's reading him: the blistered palms, the sweat, the hoarse voice. A man who spent the afternoon destroying a training dummy because he couldn't destroy the situation. A man who has grieved through his fists and arrived at the question she was waiting for.

> **ILARA:** "Sit down. Please."

He sits. She doesn't offer tea this time. This isn't a briefing. This is a lifeline.

> **ILARA:** "This morning I described the standard form of the Rite. The Complete transformation. That is the version performed on all three historical subjects. It is the simplest, the safest, and the most politically defensible."
> **EDRIN:** "But it's not the ONLY version."
> **ILARA** (a pause — the decision to speak visible in her face, the Archsorceress weighing what she's about to offer): "No. It's not."

Edrin goes very still.

> **ILARA:** "There is a second form. Older. Less commonly performed — in fact, not performed in the modern era at all, though the Silverveil texts describe it in detail. We call it the Partial Rite."
> **EDRIN** (barely breathing): "Partial."
> **EDRIN:** "Partial."
> **ILARA:** "In the Partial form, the body is feminized — breasts, hips, facial structure, skin, voice, height. All the same changes. A uterus, ovaries, and vagina are formed. You would be capable of conception, pregnancy, and childbirth. You would satisfy the succession law's requirement for a female heir."
> **EDRIN:** "But?"
> **ILARA:** "But the penis is preserved. It changes somewhat — the magic touches everything it encounters. The skin softens, the sensitivity increases, it may narrow slightly. But the organ itself remains. Functional. Present. You would possess both sets of anatomy. Female reproductive capability AND the male organ."

Edrin stares at her. The information lands in stages — disbelief, then calculation, then something he doesn't expect: HOPE. A thin, sharp thread of hope that he immediately distrusts.

> **EDRIN:** "I could... keep it."
> **ILARA:** "You could retain your penis while gaining the female anatomy required for succession. Yes."
> **EDRIN:** "And the law — the succession law would still—"
> **ILARA:** "The law requires a female heir capable of bearing children. In the Partial form, you would possess a uterus and ovaries. You would be capable of pregnancy. The presence of additional anatomy — the retained penis — does not negate the required anatomy. Legally, the Charter's conditions would be met."
> **EDRIN** (the hope sharpening, becoming dangerous — because hope in this situation is a knife that cuts both ways): "You KNEW this when I was here this morning. You sat across from me, watched me process the idea of losing my entire body, and you DIDN'T MENTION I could keep part of it."
> **ILARA** (steady, unapologetic): "Yes."
> **EDRIN:** "WHY?"
> **ILARA:** "Because if I'd told you this morning — in the first breath, alongside the Complete form — you would have seized on it without understanding the cost. You would have heard 'keep the cock' and stopped listening. I needed you to sit with the FULL loss first. To grieve it. To rage at it. To beat a training dummy until your hands bled." She glances at his blistered palms. "And THEN to come back and ask the question you just asked: 'Is there any other way?' Because now you'll listen to the COMPLICATIONS with the same seriousness you gave the Complete form. Now you understand what you're choosing BETWEEN — not just what you're choosing."

Edrin stares at her. He wants to be angry. He IS angry. But the logic is impeccable, and the manipulation was in SERVICE of his understanding, not against it. She withheld hope until he was ready to hold it carefully rather than clutch it desperately.

> **EDRIN** (through gritted teeth): "I hate that you're right."
> **ILARA:** "Most people do."
> **EDRIN:** "What complications?"
> **ILARA:** "Politically: if the Elder Council or House Ashborne learns of the retained male anatomy, they may challenge the Rite's validity. They would argue you are 'not fully transformed,' that you're still male, that the Rite was incomplete. The legal argument is defensible — the Charter says 'female heir,' not 'ONLY female.' But the debate would be vicious."
> **EDRIN:** "So I'd have to hide it."
> **ILARA:** "You would have to conceal the penis. Daily. In every public appearance. Under every gown. During every medical examination that anyone outside the Silverveil might observe. The concealment would be a constant burden — physical, emotional, political."
> **EDRIN:** "And the other complications?"
> **ILARA:** "Physically: the dual anatomy creates... complexities. Two arousal systems in one body. Hormonal interactions between the estrogenic and residual androgenic functions. Menstruation while possessing a penis. The body would be unprecedented — not in ancient history, but in MODERN history. There are no contemporary physicians who understand a body like that. The Silverveil would be your only medical resource."

She pauses. Then adds, with the precision of someone choosing every word:

> **ILARA:** "And there is one more factor. The body you'd inhabit — female with a retained penis — exists in no current social category. You would be neither the man the kingdom knew nor the woman it expects. You would be something the world doesn't have language for. The isolation of that — the loneliness of being the ONLY person with your body — is a real cost. I would be failing you if I didn't name it."

Edrin absorbs this. The hope hasn't vanished — it's been tempered. Not the wild, desperate hope of "I can keep everything!" but the cautious, measured hope of "I can keep ONE thing. The thing that matters most. At a price."

> **EDRIN:** "You said the ancient texts describe it. The Partial Rite. It's been done before?"
> **ILARA:** "The texts reference at least one subject — possibly more, the records are fragmentary. The ancient world had a word for the body the Partial Rite creates. *Vael'kirin* — 'the bridge between.' In certain old traditions, the *vael'kirin* was considered... sacred. A body that contained both creative forces — the capacity to receive life AND to project it. The magical implications are significant."
> **EDRIN:** "Sacred."
> **ILARA:** "To the old religion. The modern world is less... generous in its interpretations."
> **EDRIN** (bitterly): "The modern world would call me a freak."
> **ILARA** (firm, quiet): "The modern world doesn't know what to call you. That's different from 'freak.' The absence of a word isn't a judgment — it's an invitation to create one."

She stands. Crosses to a shelf. Returns with a scroll — old, brittle, sealed with wax.

> **ILARA:** "I won't open this now. But if you choose the Partial Rite, this scroll contains everything the Silverveil knows about the *vael'kirin* body — the care, the management, the pleasure, the magic. You would not be navigating blind. You would have guidance."

She sets the scroll on the table between them.

> **ILARA:** "The choice between the Complete and Partial forms is yours. Both are valid. Both satisfy the law. Both will transform your body in ways that cannot be undone. The Complete form takes everything and gives you a body the world understands. The Partial form takes almost everything and gives you a body the world doesn't — but lets you keep the one piece of yourself you're most afraid to lose."

Long silence.

> **EDRIN** (looking at the scroll, then at his hands, then at Ilara): "I need to think."
> **ILARA:** "You have until tomorrow morning. The Rite must be performed at the meridian hour — noon. Come to the tower by dawn to prepare. Tell me your choice then."

He stands. His legs are unsteady. He walks to the door.

> **EDRIN** (at the threshold, not turning): "If I keep it... the cock. If I walk around with breasts and a vagina and ALSO a cock... will I still be me?"
> **ILARA** (quiet, genuine): "You will be you in a body no one has ever worn before. Whether that is 'still you' depends entirely on whether YOU decide it is."

He leaves.

## THE SCENE

Edrin goes to Seraphina. Not to argue — the arguments are exhausted. To ASK.

> **EDRIN:** "Did you know? About the Partial form?"
> **SERAPHINA** (a pause — and the pause tells him: yes, she knew): "Ilara told me when we first discussed the Rite. She presented both options."
> **EDRIN:** "You didn't mention it."
> **SERAPHINA:** "I was going to. Tomorrow, if you hadn't learned of it yourself."
> **EDRIN:** "Why not YESTERDAY? When I was tearing myself apart about losing—"
> **SERAPHINA:** "Because I wanted you to confront the FULL cost first. If I'd told you immediately that you could keep your cock, you'd have seized on it without understanding what the Complete form offers. Without grieving properly. Without sitting with the loss."
> **EDRIN:** "That's MANIPULATIVE."
> **SERAPHINA** (unflinching): "That's parenting. Sometimes the same thing."

Edrin sits on the edge of her bed. The anger drains. He's too tired for anger.

> **EDRIN:** "What would you choose? If you were me?"
> **SERAPHINA** (considering — genuinely, not performing): "I can't answer that. I don't have a cock to lose."
> **EDRIN:** "Mother."
> **SERAPHINA:** "Fine. If I were you..." She pauses. Coughs. Continues. "If I were a man being asked to become a woman to save a kingdom, and I were told I could keep one piece of the man I was... I think I would agonize about it for exactly as long as you're going to agonize about it. And I think the choice would come down to one question."
> **EDRIN:** "What question?"
> **SERAPHINA:** "Not 'what do I want to keep?' but 'what am I strong enough to carry?' The Complete form is simpler. Brutal, but simple — you lose everything, you grieve, you rebuild. The Partial form... you keep one thing, but you carry the WEIGHT of it. The hiding. The politics. The loneliness of being the only person in the world with your body. The Complete form is a clean wound. The Partial form is a wound that never quite closes — but also never quite bleeds. It stays with you."

Silence.

> **SERAPHINA:** "The question isn't which is easier. Neither is easy. The question is which WEIGHT you'd rather carry. The weight of total loss? Or the weight of something preserved that must be hidden?"
> **EDRIN** (very quietly): "I don't want to lose it. My body. My cock. It's... it's the last piece of who I was born as. If I lose that, I lose EVERYTHING. Even the woman I become will have no connection to the man I was."
> **SERAPHINA:** "Your eyes won't change. Your mind won't change. Your soul—"
> **EDRIN:** "My SOUL doesn't have a cock, Mother. But my BODY does. And the body is how I know myself. How I TOUCH myself. How I exist in the physical world. If every physical trace of Edrin is erased — if the LAST PHYSICAL THING that connects me to the prince I was is dissolved into a clitoris — then Edrin is truly dead. Not transformed. DEAD."

He's crying. Not dramatically — quietly, the tears rolling, his jaw clenched against the sound.

> **SERAPHINA** (reaching for his hand — and her grip is weak, the illness eating her strength, but she holds): "Then keep it. If the cock is the thread that connects Aelindra to Edrin — if preserving it means preserving the person inside — then keep it. The political cost is real. The concealment will be constant. But the ALTERNATIVE — a child of mine who feels they've been entirely erased — that cost is higher."
> **EDRIN:** "You'd support the Partial Rite?"
> **SERAPHINA:** "I'd support whatever lets my child survive this and still recognize themselves in the mirror. If that means hiding a cock under a gown for the rest of your reign... I've hidden worse under mine."

He looks at her. She's smiling — thin, tired, genuine.

> **EDRIN:** "What have YOU hidden under a gown?"
> **SERAPHINA:** "A dagger. Three diplomatic pouches. Once, a very small cat." She pauses. "And twenty-five years of doubt that I was worthy of the crown. THAT was heavier than any cock."
> **EDRIN** (a laugh — startled, wet, cracking through the tears): "A CAT?"
> **SERAPHINA:** "It was a very important diplomatic cat. Don't ask."

They laugh together. Mother and son, for the last time. Tomorrow, if he chooses the Partial Rite, the laughter will continue — but the dynamic will shift. Son to daughter. Prince to princess. The cock preserved beneath the gown.

If he chooses the Complete form, this is the last time a mother laughs with her son. Tomorrow, she meets her daughter.

Either way: the conversation has done what Seraphina intended. Not to CHOOSE for him — but to make both choices REAL. Weighted. Understood. So that whatever he walks into the tower carrying, he carries it with open eyes.

1. **Day -2 remains unchanged.** The Summons, the Arguments, the Research — Edrin doesn't know about the Partial Rite yet on Day -2. He's learning about the Rite in general. He doesn't meet Ilara on Day -2 at all.

2. **Day -1 Midday (First Tower Visit) remains unchanged.** Ilara explains the Complete Rite ONLY. No mention of the Partial form. This is deliberate withholding — she wants him to process the full cost first.

3. **Day -1 Afternoon (Training Yard) remains unchanged.** The rage session, beating the dummy, Aldric bringing water. This is now explicitly the PROCESSING step between learning the full cost and asking "is there another way?"

4. **Day -1 Late Afternoon (NEW — Second Tower Visit) is ADDED.** After the training yard, Edrin returns to the tower. Exhausted, blistered, hoarse. He asks the question Ilara was waiting for: "Is that the ONLY form?" This is where the Partial Rite is revealed. Ilara explains why she withheld: "I needed you to sit with the full loss first. 6. **Day -1 Evening (Rowan Scene) remains unchanged.** Edrin doesn't tell Rowan the specific choice, just the general situation.

7. **Day -1 Night (Mirror Scene) gains CRITICAL subtext.** When Edrin stands before the mirror holding his cock on the last night — the scene now carries DOUBLE weight because he KNOWS he can keep it. He's not just saying goodbye. He's DECIDING. The mirror becomes a decision chamber: "Do I let go of you? Or do I carry you hidden?" The grief and the hope coexist in his grip.

8. **Day 0 choice is EARNED.** The tower-entrance choice is no longer Ilara offering a cold menu. It's Edrin DELIVERING a decision he's been wrestling with since late afternoon yesterday. He's slept on it. He's dreamed about it. He's held his cock in the dark and CHOSEN. The emotional investment is complete.

9. **The word "Vael'kirin" is planted early.** Ilara says it during the second tower visit. Players who choose the futa path will recognize the word when Kael says it on Day 13. The narrative RHYMES across weeks.

10. **Seraphina's "hiding a cat" line becomes a CALLBACK.** In the futa path, during concealment struggles, the player remembers: her mother hid a cat. And a dagger. And twenty-five years of doubt. The gown hides everything. The cock is just one more thing.

She manages what Edrin knows and WHEN he knows it to optimize his decision-making. This characterization pays off throughout the game: Ilara always has another layer, another thing she hasn't told you yet, another scroll sealed on the table waiting for the right question.
