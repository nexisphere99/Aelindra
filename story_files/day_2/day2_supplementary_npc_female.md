### NPC_D2_001: Nara — Farewell from the Tower
#### [Passage: `day2_npc_nara_farewell`]
#### Trigger: Player explores tower before crossing bridge (requires previous Nara encounters)

She's at the bottom of the stairs. Music box clipped to her belt — still playing three notes, still broken, still Nara. She's holding a small wrapped package.

"You're leaving." Not a question. She can see the determination — the set of my jaw (soft now, but the expression hasn't changed), the pack Ilara helped me assemble, the fact that I'm wearing my own ill-fitting clothes because I'd rather look absurd than wear something that acknowledges the new body.

"I'm going home."

"The tower was nice, though. Quiet. Good acoustics." She holds out the package. "I made you something. Don't open it until you're in your chambers. And don't tell Ilara — she'd say it's energetically irresponsible."

The package is light. I can feel something small and hard inside the wrapping — metal, maybe, or crystal.

"Nara. Thank you. For the tea. The biscuits. The music boxes."

"The biscuits were terrible."

"The biscuits were terrible. The kindness wasn't."

Her brown eyes go bright. She blinks rapidly. "Go. Before I cry and short-circuit the sympathetic resonance experiment, which I ALMOST have working and I can't afford the emotional interference."

I cross the bridge. Behind me, three notes play, growing fainter with distance.

**Later, in chambers — opening the package:**

A small crystal sphere, about the size of a walnut. When I hold it, it warms in my palm. And from inside — faintly, tinily — three notes. The same three notes. Playing on a loop.

The sympathetic resonance. She got it working. Across the distance between the tower and the palace, the crystal resonates with her music box. Three notes, transmitted through enchanted crystal, playing in my palm.

She gave me a piece of the tower to carry. A signal that says *I'm here, I'm here, I'm here* — in a language made of magic and bad baking and earnest, uncomplicated love.

**[Stress -8, RES +2, Nara Familiarity +3, Item: Resonance Crystal]**

## LOCATION: PALACE CORRIDORS

### NPC_D2_002: The Page Boy Who Walked Into a Wall
#### [Passage: `day2_npc_pageboy`]
#### Trigger: Player traverses corridors (auto-encounter during gauntlet)

He's waiting. Or rather — he's been posted at the junction near the east wing with a message for someone, and when I round the corner, he's the one who walked into the wall, and his forehead has a red mark, and his companions have abandoned him, and he's standing alone looking like he wants the flagstones to swallow him.

He sees me. Goes crimson. Opens his mouth. Closes it.

"You're the one who met the wall," I say. My voice — the woman's voice — carrying the same dry tone that Edrin used. The same words Edrin would have chosen.

"I — Your Highness — I'm so sorry, I didn't — I wasn't expecting — you look very—" He's fourteen. He's terrified. He's stammering with the desperate incoherence of a teenager who has done something embarrassing in front of royalty and is trying to die of shame but his body won't cooperate.

"Different?"

"Beautiful," he blurts. Then goes so red that the wall-bruise disappears into the general flush. "I mean — different! I meant different! I didn't — oh no—"

Despite everything — the gauntlet, the stares, the woman with the brush, the corridors that feel different at five foot six — I laugh. A small, real laugh. The sound surprises both of us. It's higher than Edrin's laugh. But the humour behind it is the same: the ability to find something funny in the middle of a disaster.

"What's your name?"

"Tobin, Your Highness. Page to Lord Merrick. I'm — I'm very sorry about the wall."

"The wall will survive. Tell Lord Merrick I said his page has good reflexes, if not spatial awareness."

He bows — deep, grateful, the bow of a fourteen-year-old who has just been forgiven by someone he accidentally offended and who is now going to tell every page in the palace that the new princess is actually funny. The gossip will spread. The story will become legend. The page who walked into a wall because the princess was pretty. It's not the worst reputation to begin with.

### NPC_D2_003: Lady Harwick — The Perfumed Assessment
#### [Passage: `day2_npc_harwick`]
#### Trigger: Player explores corridors (MIDDAY or AFTERNOON)

She materialises from a doorway like a silk-wrapped ambush. The same cloud of perfume. The same jewellery. The same smile that could mean seventeen different things simultaneously.

"Princess Aelindra." The name, used publicly, for the first time by someone other than Ilara. It hits my ears and detonates somewhere behind my sternum. "What a TRANSFORMATION. And I don't mean that flippantly, dear — well, perhaps a little flippantly. You look absolutely striking."

"Lady Harwick."

"You have your mother's cheekbones. I always thought Edrin had his father's structure — strong jaw, broad shoulders. But the cheekbones were always Seraphina's. Now they're YOURS, and darling, they're magnificent."

She's assessing me. The compliments are genuine but the eyes behind them are calculating — Lady Harwick doesn't offer a word that isn't strategic. She's evaluating the new princess the way she evaluates everything: as political material.

"The court is buzzing, of course. Lord Merrick is drafting a formal announcement. The Elder Council has requested — well, I say 'requested,' they 'demanded' is closer — a presentation. They want to SEE you. Confirm the transformation. Verify that the succession is secured."

"When?"

"Lord Merrick suggests Day Seven. Your mother agrees. One week from the Rite — enough time for the body to settle, for you to learn basic court protocol in the new form, for the gown to be fitted."

"I'm not wearing a gown."

"Darling." She pats my arm. Her rings dig into the sleeve. "You'll wear whatever makes the Elder Council confirm you as heir. If that's a gown, you'll wear a gown. If it's armour, you'll wear armour. Personally, I'd recommend the gown — you have the waist for it, and there's nothing the Council respects more than a woman who looks the part while being smarter than all of them combined."

She adjusts a brooch. Looks at me. Behind the perfume and the jewellery and the strategic smiling, something else — warmth, maybe. Or the recognition of one woman by another.

"Your mother was terrified on her coronation day. She told me later — years later — that her knees were shaking so badly she was grateful the gown was floor-length. Nobody saw. She looked like a queen. She BECAME a queen. The knees stopped shaking eventually."

She glides away. Trailing perfume. The last thing I hear: "Eat something, dear. You're thin."

**[AUT +2 (political intelligence — Council presentation upcoming), GRA +1, Stress +5 (presentation pressure), Reputation (Court) +2]**

### NPC_D2_004: Ser Caedric — The Bench Observes
#### [Passage: `day2_npc_caedric`]
#### Trigger: Player passes portrait gallery (requires previous Caedric encounters)

The bench. The knight. The rusted sword. Unchanged. The most stable element in a palace that has been rearranged by my transformation.

He doesn't stand. He never stands. But he looks at me — and for the first time since Day -2, his expression shifts into something I haven't seen before. Not surprise (he knew). Not calculation (he's too old for politics). Something softer. Recognition.

"There she is."

Two words. Present tense. Not *there he is* — *there she is.* As if I arrived, rather than changed. As if the person sitting on this bench for twelve years was waiting for THIS version of me to appear, and now that I have, the waiting is over.

"Ser Caedric."

"Sit."

I sit. The bench is the same — warm from his presence, worn smooth. I'm smaller on it. My feet are further from the ground. The portraits of queens above us look down, and for the first time, they're looking at someone who belongs in their line.

"You look like your grandmother," he says. "Around the eyes. And the jaw — she had that same set. The 'I dare you to comment' jaw. She used it on everyone from the Elder Council to the stable master."

"Did it work?"

"The Council confirmed her succession in three minutes. The stable master still gave her a difficult horse. Some things don't respond to jaws."

He adjusts his sword belt. The leather creaks. The sound is the same sound it's been making for twelve years.

"You're scared."

"I'm terrified."

"Good. Your grandmother was terrified. Your mother was terrified. Every queen in those portraits was terrified on the day the crown touched her head. The ones who WEREN'T terrified were the bad ones." He looks up at the portraits. "Fear means you understand what's at stake. Fearlessness is just ignorance in armour."

He pats the bench beside me. The wood, worn smooth. The same bench.

"I'll be here. Same bench. Same sword. Same stubbornness."

**[RES +3, Stress -8, Caedric bond maintained]**

### NPC_D2_005: Marta — The Stool
#### [Passage: `day2_npc_marta`]
#### Trigger: Player visits Kitchen on Day 2

The kitchen is running at normal tempo — not crisis tempo, not siege tempo. Normal. Which means Marta has decided that the crisis is manageable and the appropriate response is regular output rather than panic baking. This is, paradoxically, more reassuring than the cheese pastries.

She sees me in the doorway. For one second — one fraction of a second — her face does something. Not shock. Not pity. Something that passes too fast to name. Grief, maybe, for the boy. Joy, maybe, for the survival. Love, certainly, for the person regardless.

Then: "Sit."

She points at the stool. The stool. My stool. The same stool I've been sitting on since I was nine years old, since I was small enough that my legs swung free and Marta had to put a box beneath for my feet.

I sit. My legs don't swing — I'm not tall enough now for them to hang quite right, but I'm not short enough for the box. I'm in between. I'm in between everything.

She puts food in front of me without asking. Bread. Cheese. Soup — thick, onion and thyme, the recipe that has been unchanged since before I was born. Two cheese pastries. An apple pastry.

"Eat."

I eat. The flavour is the same. The jaw chews differently. The throat swallows differently. But the food is Marta's food and Marta's food tastes the same regardless of the body consuming it because Marta's food operates on a level that transcends the merely physical.

She watches me eat. The wooden spoon is in her hand — always, perpetually, the extension of her arm and her authority. She doesn't comment on the body. She doesn't mention the breasts or the hips or the voice or the face. She watches me eat the way she's watched me eat since I was nine: with the fierce, protective attention of a woman who believes that feeding someone is the highest form of care.

"Same stool," she says, when I've finished. "Same pastries."

"Same Marta."

"Damn right." She takes the plate. Returns to her domain. Then, over her shoulder: "You need to eat more. You're burning through food — the body is still changing. I'll have extra trays sent to your chambers. Three meals isn't enough."

"Marta, I can't eat four—"

"Five. Three meals and two snacks. The body wants what the body wants. Don't argue with the body OR with me. We both win."

She's right. The hunger is constant — a low-grade demand that rises to a roar if I go more than three hours without food. The transformation is still consuming energy. The breasts are still growing. The body is still building itself and it needs raw materials and Marta is going to provide them whether I request it or not.

The stool is the same. The pastries are the same. The love is the same.

Some things survive the Rite.

**[Energy +15, Stress -10, Marta bond maintained, Nutrition protocol established]**

### NPC_D2_006: Handmaiden Maren — The First Dressing
#### [Passage: `day2_npc_maren`]
#### Trigger: After Maren delivers clothing (main scene), player interacts with "Maren" again

She's tidying. Folding the men's clothes I discarded, hanging them in the wardrobe with the care of someone who understands that these garments are relics, not rubbish.

"Your Highness... may I speak freely?"

"You're folding my dead wardrobe. I think we've passed the formality threshold."

She smiles. Small, careful. "I was assigned to you because Mistress Alva said I was 'least likely to stare or say something idiotic.' I think that was a compliment."

"From Alva, that's practically a marriage proposal."

She sets down the shirt she's folding. Looks at me — directly, without the sidelong glances the corridor servants deployed. Direct. Honest.

"I've served three ladies at court. Lady Veltham, Lady Thornecrest's daughter, and the Tormark ambassador's wife. I know how to dress a woman, manage a wardrobe, assist with bathing and grooming, and maintain discretion about private matters." She pauses. "I also know that you didn't ask for any of this. And I know that having a stranger help you into a breast support garment is probably not how you imagined your week going."

"It was not at the top of the list, no."

"Then I'll make it as painless as possible. I'm here to help, not to observe. Whatever you need — clothing adjustments, hair management, questions about the body that you don't want to ask the Archsorceress because she's too clinical, or questions you don't want to ask anyone because they're too embarrassing — I'm here."

"Questions about the body?"

"Lady Veltham's daughter was fourteen when I served her. She had a LOT of questions about the body. I answered all of them without judgment. I'll extend you the same courtesy, Your Highness."

The offer is genuine. And the comparison — me to a fourteen-year-old girl going through puberty — is simultaneously insulting and completely accurate. I'm going through puberty. Second puberty, compressed and magical, but puberty nonetheless. The breasts, the hormones, the emotional volatility, the body changing faster than the mind can track. I'm a twenty-year-old woman experiencing fourteen-year-old milestones at ten times the speed.

"Maren. The breast support garment. The one you helped me with."

"Yes?"

"Is it supposed to itch?"

"Slightly, at first. The linen needs to soften — it's new. After three washings, it'll feel like a second skin. Would you like me to wash it tonight? I can have it ready by morning."

"Please."

And like that — with a question about itchy linen and a practical answer about washing cycles — the relationship begins. Not dramatic. Not emotional. Just a woman asking another woman about the mechanics of wearing a bra, and the other woman answering, and the normalcy of the exchange being its own kind of healing.

**[GRA +1 (feminine knowledge), Stress -3, Maren Familiarity +3]**

### NPC_D2_007: The Queen's Visit — A Note Under the Door
#### [Passage: `day2_npc_queen_note2`]
#### Trigger: EVENING — another note arrives

Another note. Same seal. Mother's handwriting.

*Tomorrow I come to you. Be ready to learn. The kingdom won't wait for your grief to finish — and neither can I. But we'll grieve together, between the lessons. I promise.*

*Wear something that fits. You represent the crown now.*

*I love you. I'm proud of you. I'm sorry.*

*— S.*

Three sentences of statecraft. Three sentences of love. One sentence of apology. Seraphina in seven lines — the queen, the mother, and the woman who knows what she's done.

*Wear something that fits.* A command and a plea. Stop drowning in Edrin's clothes. Stop pretending the body isn't real. The kingdom is watching and the kingdom needs to see a princess, not a refugee from someone else's wardrobe.

*I'm sorry.* Two words she's never said before. Not during the arguments, not during the reveal, not during the farewell. She waited until the Rite was done and the body was changed and the cost was paid before she allowed herself the luxury of remorse.

I put the note under my pillow. Next to the other one. A growing collection of maternal love compressed into parchment.

**[Queen Trust +3, Stress +5 then -3 (net +2), Objective: Queen's visit tomorrow (Day 3)]**

### NPC_D2_008: Night Guard Perrin — New Rounds
#### [Passage: `day2_npc_perrin`]
#### Trigger: NIGHT — player interacts with door (requires previous Perrin encounters)

The same corridor. The same post. Perrin.

He looks at me when I open the door. The same attentive gaze. The same earnest, uncalculating attention.

"Your Highness." No hesitation. No stumble. No false start. The address delivered with the same inflection he used three days ago, as if nothing has changed except the person being addressed.

"Perrin."

"Quiet night. Though — your friend is playing again. The guest wing. Softer tonight."

Rowan. Playing in the guest wing. Not at my door — giving me space. But playing. Present. The lute reaching through stone walls and night air to say what words don't need to: *still here.*

"Perrin. Do I look different to you?"

He considers this. The same careful consideration, the same unhurried processing that I valued on Day -2.

"You look like the same person in different clothes. The eyes are the same. The way you lean on the doorframe — that's the same. You used to lean left shoulder first. You still do."

I am, in fact, leaning on the doorframe. Left shoulder first. A posture I didn't choose, a lean I didn't plan. The body changed but the habits didn't.

"The house is different," he says. "But the person living in it leans the same way."

The body's the house. The person's the tenant. He said it on Day -2 and he's saying it now, and the consistency — the beautiful, stubborn consistency of a night guard who believes the same things on Thursday that he believed on Monday — is the anchor I didn't know I needed.

"Good night, Perrin."

"Good night, Your Highness. Sleep well."

I close the door. The lute plays, distantly. Perrin stands his post. The ceiling crack waits.

**[RES +2, Stress -5, Perrin Familiarity maintained]**

### NPC_D2_009: Old Wynn — New Growth
#### [Passage: `day2_npc_wynn`]
#### Trigger: Player explores gardens (requires previous Wynn encounters)

He's at the jasmine. Trowel in hand. The new growth is visible — green shoots pushing outward from where the dead branches were cut. Different directions. Unexpected angles. The plant remaking itself.

He doesn't turn when I approach. He knows my footstep — or rather, he knows SOMETHING is different about it, and the difference doesn't concern him because people change and footsteps change and the garden doesn't judge.

"New growth," he says. "Four shoots. Healthy. Vigorous. Going in directions I didn't predict."

"Does that bother you?"

"Does it bother the jasmine?" He stands. Looks at me. The sharp old eyes take in everything — the new body, the women's clothes, the face, the height. He looks the way he looks at a plant: assessing health, noting changes, predicting growth.

"You're growing too," he says. "I can see it. The face is settling. The body is filling." He touches a jasmine shoot — the newest one, pushing outward toward the morning sun. "Don't fight the direction. Let the growth go where it goes. The jasmine doesn't argue with its own branches."

He hands me a cutting. Fresh jasmine. Three white flowers, opening.

"For the new room. Whatever the room looks like now."

**[RES +2, Stress -5, Wynn bond maintained, Item: Fresh jasmine cutting]**
